A PERSONAL CREATOR WORKSPACE

Create thoughtfully.
Understand the results.

Yasron brings video preparation, approved publishing and performance records into a local workspace operated by its creator.

TikTok integration · Sandbox testing
01

Prepare

Organize video outputs, captions and platform-specific publishing details. Review the content and destination accounts before sending.

02

Connect

The operator authorizes accounts they own. The TikTok integration is being tested with designated Sandbox users; there is no public registration on this website.

03

Understand

Read authorized account identity and available public-video performance counters, such as views, likes, comments and shares. Availability depends on the platform and granted permissions.

How data moves

The workspace runs on the operator’s computer. It stores connection credentials and performance records locally. Approved publishing can use Buffer and Cloudinary; direct TikTok access is used for authorized data reading. This static website explains the project and its policies; it does not collect social-account credentials or host the workspace.

Yasron is an independent project maintained by yasaraydemir, not an official TikTok, Meta, Google or Buffer product.
